About the app

Passcard Store is a free, open-source password and card manager built for people who want full control of their data. It works entirely on-device — your secrets never leave your phone.

🛡️

Encrypted at rest

Every password, card number and CVV is encrypted with AES-256. The data key is protected by your PIN and never stored in the clear.

📴

Truly offline

No account, no servers, no analytics or ads. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

🔑

PIN & biometrics

Unlock with a 4 or 6-digit PIN or your fingerprint/face. Auto-lock and clipboard auto-clear keep things private.

💳

Passwords & cards

Store logins and credit/debit cards with categories, search and a strong password generator.

📦

Encrypted backups

Create AES-256 encrypted .vaultx backups, or export/import via CSV — you decide where they go.

⏱️

Auto-lock & clear

The vault locks in the background and copied secrets are wiped from the clipboard automatically.

🧩

Open source

The full source code is available on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can audit it.
